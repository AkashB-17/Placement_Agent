Northbridge Institute of Technology
Campus Placement Policy for Graduating Batch of 2027

Clause 1: Academic Eligibility
Students must maintain a minimum Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 7.0 on a 10.0 scale up to the penultimate semester to be eligible for the placement process.

Clause 2: Active Backlogs
Students must have zero active backlogs at the time of applying for any company. Cleared backlogs from previous semesters are acceptable, provided the updated transcript reflects the clearance before the application deadline.

Clause 3: Eligible Branches
Participation in the placement drive is strictly restricted to students enrolled in the following undergraduate degree programs: Computer Science and Engineering, Information Technology, Electronics and Communication Engineering, and Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

Clause 4: Batch Restriction
The upcoming campus placement drive is exclusively for the 2027 graduating batch. Alumni from previous batches or students expected to graduate in subsequent years are not eligible.

Clause 5: Application Limits
To ensure fair opportunity for all candidates, a student may hold a maximum of 10 active applications at any given time. An application is considered active until the company announces the final selection results for that specific drive.

Clause 6: Placed Student Policy
Once a student secures a job offer through the campus placement cell, they are automatically deregistered from applying to further drives. The only exception is for dream-company drives, which are defined as companies offering a Cost to Company (CTC) of at least twice (2x) the student's current secured offer.

Clause 7: Conduct and Offer Acceptance
Students are expected to maintain professional conduct during pre-placement talks, interviews, and assessments. Any instance of malpractice or unexcused absence from a scheduled interview will result in immediate debarment from the placement process. Furthermore, students must accept the first job offer they receive unless eligible for a dream-company upgrade. Declining an offer without a valid, documented emergency will lead to permanent suspension from all placement activities.
