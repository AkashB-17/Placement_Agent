Job Title: HR Intern

About the Company:
PeopleFirst Corp is an innovative human resources management firm that provides talent acquisition, employee engagement, and payroll solutions to mid-sized enterprises. We believe that people are the most valuable asset of any organization. Our internal culture is vibrant, inclusive, and heavily focused on employee well-being and continuous professional development.

Role Overview & Responsibilities:
We are looking for an enthusiastic HR Intern to support our talent acquisition and employee engagement teams. This role will give you hands-on experience in the entire employee lifecycle, from sourcing candidates to organizing company-wide events. You will need strong interpersonal skills and a highly organized approach to manage multiple administrative tasks efficiently.

* Assist in drafting job descriptions and posting them on various job boards.
* Screen candidate resumes and schedule initial phone interviews.
* Coordinate with hiring managers to schedule technical interview rounds.
* Maintain and update employee records in the HR Information System.
* Help organize monthly employee engagement activities and team-building events.
* Answer basic employee queries regarding leave policies and benefits.

Required Skills:

* Interpersonal skills
* Recruitment basics
* MS Office
* Communication

Preferred Skills:

* Event management
* Public speaking
* Canva

Eligibility Criteria:

* Minimum CGPA: None. We value communication skills over academic grades for this role.
* Allowed Branches: all branches.
* Batch: 2027.
* A proactive attitude and a willingness to learn HR operations.

Compensation and Perks:

* Stipend: ₹25,000 per month during the 6-month internship, transitioning to a CTC of 4.5 LPA upon full-time conversion.
* Location: Mumbai, Maharashtra.
* Mentorship from senior HR leaders and networking opportunities.

Application Details:
Apply on the placement portal with a well-formatted resume. The interview process will involve a group discussion, a situational judgment interview to test interpersonal skills, and a final interview with the HR Director.
