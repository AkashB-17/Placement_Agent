Company Name: TalentBridge HR Services

Job Title: HR Intern
Job Type: Internship (6 months, PPO opportunity)
Experience: Freshers

About the Company:
TalentBridge HR Services provides end-to-end recruitment, campus hiring, and employee engagement solutions to high-growth technology startups across India. Our team manages high-volume hiring drives, employer branding campaigns, and onboarding programs. We offer interns real ownership from week one in a friendly, collaborative office environment.

Role Overview & Responsibilities:
As an HR Intern, you will support our talent acquisition specialists in sourcing candidates, coordinating interview schedules, and ensuring a smooth onboarding experience for new hires. You will also assist in organizing internal engagement events and maintaining accurate candidate trackers.

* Source and screen entry-level candidate profiles across job portals and campus networks.
* Coordinate interview calendars between candidates and technical hiring panels.
* Follow up with shortlisted applicants to collect onboarding documents and feedback.
* Maintain daily hiring trackers, pipeline metrics, and attendance sheets in spreadsheets.
* Support the people operations team in planning monthly town halls and team activities.
* Draft clear, professional email communications for candidates and internal stakeholders.

Required Skills:

* Recruitment coordination
* MS Excel
* Communication
* Onboarding support

Preferred Skills:

* LinkedIn Recruiter
* Canva

Eligibility Criteria:

* Minimum CGPA: None.
* Allowed Branches: all branches.
* Batch: graduating in 2027.
* Strong organizational skills and a people-first attitude are required.

Compensation and Perks:

* Stipend: ₹20,000 per month (PPO CTC of 4.5 LPA based on internship performance).
* Location: Noida, Uttar Pradesh.
* Five-day work week, mentorship from HR leads, and internship completion certificate.

Application Details:
Interested students should apply on the campus placement portal. The selection process consists of resume shortlisting, a group discussion on workplace scenarios, and a personal interview with the Talent Acquisition Lead.
Deadline: December 14, 2026.
