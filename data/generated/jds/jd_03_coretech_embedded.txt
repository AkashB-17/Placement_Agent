Role Overview & Responsibilities:
We are hiring an Embedded/Firmware Engineer to design and develop low-level software for our next-generation microcontrollers. You will be working at the intersection of hardware and software, bringing silicon to life. This role requires deep knowledge of computer architecture and real-time processing.

* Write efficient and reliable firmware for 32-bit and 64-bit microcontrollers.
* Debug hardware-software integration issues using oscilloscopes and logic analyzers.
* Develop device drivers for various sensors and communication modules.
* Optimize power consumption for battery-operated IoT devices.
* Participate in schematic reviews and provide software-side hardware requirements.
* Maintain version control for all firmware builds and releases.
