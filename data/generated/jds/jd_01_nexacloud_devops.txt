About the Company:
NexaCloud Solutions is a rapidly growing technology startup specializing in scalable cloud infrastructure and enterprise deployment strategies. We partner with global clients to transition their legacy systems into robust, cloud-native environments. Our engineering culture promotes continuous learning, agile methodologies, and automation at every layer. We are looking for passionate individuals who want to build the future of cloud computing.

Role Overview & Responsibilities:
As a Cloud/DevOps Engineer, you will be an integral part of our infrastructure team. You will work closely with senior engineers to automate deployment pipelines, monitor system health, and troubleshoot production issues. Your daily tasks will include writing automation scripts, configuring cloud services, and participating in architecture discussions.

* Assist in the design and maintenance of CI/CD pipelines.
* Monitor cloud infrastructure usage and optimize for cost and performance.
* Write infrastructure-as-code scripts for automated provisioning.
* Collaborate with development teams to ensure smooth code deployments.
* Participate in on-call rotations and resolve infrastructure alerts.
* Document system configurations and operational procedures.
