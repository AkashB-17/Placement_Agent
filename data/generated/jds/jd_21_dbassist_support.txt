Job Title: Technical Support Engineer (SQL)

About the Company:
DBAssist Enterprise delivers managed database operations and high-availability support for retail, healthcare, and ERP platforms. Our support engineering center in Chennai ensures that mission-critical databases stay fast, consistent, and online round the clock. We train fresh graduates thoroughly in relational database internals and enterprise incident management.

Role Overview & Responsibilities:
As a Technical Support Engineer (SQL), you will investigate customer-reported data discrepancies, slow-running queries, and application timeouts. You will partner with client IT teams over calls and tickets to diagnose root causes and deliver timely fixes within agreed SLAs.

* Triage and resolve incoming Level-1 and Level-2 database support tickets within SLA timelines.
* Write and optimize SQL queries to inspect records, audit transactions, and fix data issues.
* Monitor database health metrics, disk utilization, and replication lag on Linux servers.
* Explain technical root causes and workarounds clearly to non-technical client teams.
* Escalate complex software defects to product engineering with complete reproduction steps.
* Maintain runbooks and update the customer-facing troubleshooting knowledge base.
