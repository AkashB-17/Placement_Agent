About the Company:
SecureSphere Cyber provides elite security consulting and managed security services to financial institutions and government agencies. We specialize in threat intelligence, penetration testing, and incident response. Our mission is to protect our clients' most sensitive data from advanced persistent threats and zero-day vulnerabilities. We pride ourselves on having some of the sharpest security minds in the industry.

Role Overview & Responsibilities:
The Cybersecurity Analyst will join our Security Operations Center (SOC) team. You will monitor networks for malicious activity, analyze security alerts, and assist in vulnerability assessments. This is a high-responsibility role where attention to detail and rapid response times are critical.

* Monitor client networks continuously for security breaches and unauthorized access.
* Investigate security alerts generated by SIEM tools and firewalls.
* Perform routine vulnerability scans on web applications and infrastructure.
* Document security incidents and assist in post-incident forensic analysis.
* Stay updated on the latest cyber threats, malware strains, and hacking techniques.
* Educate client employees on security best practices and phishing awareness.
