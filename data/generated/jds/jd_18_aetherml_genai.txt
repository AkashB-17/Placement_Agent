About the Company:
AetherML Labs builds domain-specific generative AI assistants for legal, healthcare, and enterprise knowledge management. Our engineering team focuses on retrieval-augmented generation, model quantization, and low-latency inference pipelines. We operate with a flat hierarchy where fresh graduates ship production models within their first quarter.

Role Overview & Responsibilities:
As an ML/GenAI Engineer, you will design, evaluate, and deploy LLM-powered applications that answer complex enterprise queries with high factual accuracy. You will work on document chunking strategies, embedding optimization, and automated evaluation suites to minimize hallucinations in production.

* Build and maintain retrieval-augmented generation pipelines over large unstructured document stores.
* Experiment with open-weight language models and fine-tuning techniques for domain adaptation.
* Design automated evaluation benchmarks to track response quality, latency, and token cost.
* Manage vector indexes and tune similarity search parameters for high recall.
* Collaborate with backend engineers to expose AI workflows as scalable microservices.
* Stay current with recent NLP and generative AI research and prototype promising approaches.
