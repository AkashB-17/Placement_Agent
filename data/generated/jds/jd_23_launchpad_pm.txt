About the Company:
LaunchPad Digital is a fast-growing fintech and personal wealth startup serving over three million young Indian investors. We design intuitive mobile-first tools for mutual fund tracking, goal-based saving, and automated tax harvesting. Our product team is deeply metrics-driven and obsessed with simplifying complex financial workflows.

Role Overview & Responsibilities:
As a Product Management Trainee, you will rotate across our onboarding, engagement, and payments squads during your first year. You will support Product Managers in conducting user interviews, analyzing funnel drop-offs, writing detailed user stories, and coordinating feature launches with engineering and design.

* Analyze user funnel metrics and retention cohorts to uncover product friction points.
* Conduct structured user research calls and usability tests with new and existing customers.
* Draft clear product requirement documents, acceptance criteria, and release checklists.
* Partner with product designers to review prototypes and iterate on user flows.
* Track post-launch feature adoption and present sprint impact summaries to leadership.
* Maintain the product backlog and assist in prioritizing bug fixes and enhancements.
