Job Title: Business Development Associate

About the Company:
GrowthPulse Media is a B2B digital marketing and analytics agency helping mid-market SaaS and D2C brands scale their customer acquisition. With teams in Mumbai, Delhi NCR, and Bengaluru, we combine performance marketing with data-backed sales outreach. We are looking for energetic, target-driven freshers who want to build a fast-paced career in enterprise sales.

Role Overview & Responsibilities:
As a Business Development Associate, you will own the top-of-funnel sales pipeline by researching target accounts, pitching our services to decision-makers, and closing introductory discovery calls. You will work closely with account executives to craft tailored proposals and negotiate commercial terms with prospective clients.

* Research prospective B2B accounts and identify key marketing and growth decision-makers.
* Conduct outbound outreach via cold calls, personalized emails, and professional networks.
* Lead initial discovery conversations to understand client pain points and budget constraints.
* Negotiate service packages and follow up consistently to move deals through the funnel.
* Maintain accurate records of all client interactions and pipeline stages in the CRM.
* Prepare weekly conversion reports and share market feedback with the strategy team.

* Market research

* Minimum CGPA: No CGPA cut-off.

Compensation and Perks:
