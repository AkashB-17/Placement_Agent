About the Company:
DataVeda Analytics is a premier data science consultancy firm helping Fortune 500 companies unlock the power of their data. We build cutting-edge predictive models, recommendation engines, and business intelligence dashboards. Our team is composed of top-tier mathematicians, statisticians, and software engineers working together to solve complex data challenges across the retail, finance, and healthcare sectors.

Role Overview & Responsibilities:
The Data Scientist role is tailored for analytical minds who love deriving insights from unstructured data. You will work on real-world datasets to build machine learning models that drive business decisions. You will be mentored by lead data scientists and given the opportunity to present your findings to clients.

* Clean, process, and analyze large volumes of structured and unstructured data.
* Build and train machine learning models for predictive analytics.
* Create interactive data visualizations to explain model outputs to non-technical stakeholders.
* Collaborate with data engineers to deploy models into production environments.
* Keep up-to-date with the latest research in statistical modeling and machine learning.
* Conduct A/B testing to measure the impact of algorithm changes.
