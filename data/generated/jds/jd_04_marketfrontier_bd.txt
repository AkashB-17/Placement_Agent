About the Company:
MarketFrontier Global is a fast-growing B2B SaaS platform that helps enterprises streamline their procurement and supply chain operations. With offices across Asia and Europe, we are expanding our market reach and looking for high-energy individuals to drive our sales initiatives. We believe in nurturing talent and providing accelerated career paths for top performers in our business development teams.

Role Overview & Responsibilities:
As a Business Development Executive, you will be the face of MarketFrontier Global for our prospective clients. You will be responsible for identifying new business opportunities, understanding client needs, and pitching our SaaS solutions. This role requires high emotional intelligence, resilience, and excellent communication abilities.

* Conduct market research to identify potential enterprise clients.
* Reach out to leads via cold calling, emails, and professional networks.
* Qualify leads and schedule product demonstration meetings for senior sales managers.
* Maintain and update the sales pipeline in the CRM system.
* Build and nurture long-term relationships with key client stakeholders.
* Gather market intelligence and competitor analysis to assist the product team.

Required Skills:

* Client communication
* Negotiation
* Lead generation
* CRM

* Presentation skills
